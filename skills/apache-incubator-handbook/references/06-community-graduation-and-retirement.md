# Community health, graduation, and retirement

Last verified: 2026-08-02.

## Healthy incubation

Incubation is not only license cleanup or infrastructure migration. A podling is
learning to become a self-governing Apache community.

Useful evidence includes:

- decisions and technical disagreement occurring openly and respectfully;
- contributors from more than one employer or organization;
- regular recognition of sustained contributors as committers/PPMC members;
- release, reporting, website, licensing, and infrastructure knowledge shared
  across several people rather than held by one founder;
- active Mentors who guide without operating the project on its behalf;
- plans and outcomes recorded on public mailing lists;
- the ability to identify and address legal, community, and governance risks.

Contributor counts alone do not prove diversity. Graduation guidance looks for
at least three legally independent committers and no single person or entity
whose absence would make the project unable to govern or continue.

## Graduation readiness

Before starting graduation discussion, the podling should be able to show:

- incubation status tasks are complete and current;
- name and trademark questions are resolved;
- code provenance, grants, licensing, and release practice are understood;
- it has prepared, voted, and published compliant ASF releases;
- the website and public identity meet Apache/Incubator requirements;
- an open, diverse, meritocratic, self-sustaining community exists;
- the PPMC can make decisions, add people, report, resolve conflict, and operate
  without dependence on Mentors or one employer;
- Mentors and the IPMC have no unresolved blocking concerns.

Use the ASF maturity model as a self-assessment aid, not as a box-counting
substitute for community evidence.

## Graduation process

1. PPMC and Mentors perform a readiness assessment and prepare evidence.
2. Start `[DISCUSS]` on `general@incubator.apache.org`, normally including a
   proposed Board resolution and the intended destination TLP/PMC.
3. Resolve concerns raised during discussion.
4. Hold an IPMC graduation `[VOTE]` on the general list. It uses majority
   approval and normally remains open at least 72 hours.
5. After a successful result, the IPMC submits the resolution to the ASF Board.
6. The Board establishes the TLP; then complete post-graduation infrastructure,
   branding, roster, reporting, website, distribution, and Mentor-offboarding
   work.

The IPMC vote recommends graduation; the Board creates the TLP.

## Retirement

Retirement means the podling community is no longer continuing in the
Incubator; it does not claim that the code is unusable.

Normal path:

1. Discuss the situation openly on the podling dev list.
2. Hold an appropriate community vote or consensus process.
3. Ask the IPMC on `general@incubator.apache.org` to ratify retirement, normally
   by lazy consensus.
4. Update podling metadata/status, remove current dist areas, preserve eligible
   historical releases through the archive, mark repositories retired/read-only,
   close or freeze infrastructure, and resolve any outstanding copyright issue.

If copyright/provenance was never resolved, affected code may not be eligible
to remain in ASF version control.
