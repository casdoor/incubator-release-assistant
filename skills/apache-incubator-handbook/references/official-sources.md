## Live-state exception

Offline summaries cannot answer these without current evidence:

- who is currently a Mentor, PPMC member, IPMC member, or committer;
- the current month's report schedule/template and whether sign-off is complete;
- whether a mailing-list vote has opened, closed, or changed tally;
- current files in dist-dev/dist-release/downloads;
- current policy text after this handbook's verification date.

For those questions, verify the official roster, mailing-list archive, report
reminder/wiki, distribution endpoint, or policy page requested by the user.
